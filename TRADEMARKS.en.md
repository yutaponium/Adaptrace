# Use of the name and logo

[日本語](TRADEMARKS.md) | English

> The Japanese version is the original. This translation follows it (ADR-003).

Adaptrace's code is published under the [MIT License](LICENSE).
The name "Adaptrace" and its logo ([docs/images/logo/](docs/images/logo/)), however, are not covered by the MIT License.
The name and logo are not registered trademarks. Please follow the policy below, so that users can tell which Adaptrace is the original.

## You may, without asking

- Introduce and mention Adaptrace. You may use the name and logo in articles, talks, blog posts, comparisons and the like
- Say that something works with Adaptrace, or was made with it. Examples: "Supports Adaptrace bundles", "A plugin for Adaptrace"
- Redistribute the contents of this repository without changes

## Please do not

- Use the Adaptrace name and logo for a fork or a derivative work. Give it a different name and logo. Saying "based on Adaptrace" is fine
- Use the Adaptrace name in the name of a product, service, company or domain
- Use them in a way that suggests the original project endorses you or is affiliated with you
- Change the shape or colors of the logo. Resizing it, and choosing the version for light or dark backgrounds, are fine

## Contact

For any use not covered above, contact [adaptrace.project@gmail.com](mailto:adaptrace.project@gmail.com).
